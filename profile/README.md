**The 561 Group.** The union of the four organizations below, operated by Morgan Sennhauser.

The 561 Group is operated by Morgan Sennhauser. Its work moves from science to engineering to operations to public
services, and each step has its own organization:

| Organization | Register | Default license |
|---|---|---|
| [Lenticule Science](https://github.com/lenticule-science) | Science: investigates what is possible, and publishes original investigations, sources, methods and reproducible results, supporting each claim to the level appropriate to its use. | MIT |
| [Red Cup Engineering](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering) | Engineering: makes the actual components, their composition boundaries, build inputs, tests and demonstrations independently reproducible. | MIT |
| [Coesite Operations](https://github.com/coesite-operations) | Operations: runs what engineering builds, and releases truthful runbooks, limits, hazards and recovery procedures. | Apache-2.0 |
| [Causeway Services](https://github.com/causeway-services) | Public services: gives people usable free-software rights and practical ways to learn, operate, challenge, contribute and benefit. | Apache-2.0 |
| [561 Group](https://github.com/561-group) | The union of the four organizations below, operated by Morgan Sennhauser. | 561 Group Repository License 1.0 (all rights reserved) |

## Ways to participate economically

**Sustain public work:** [Support the 561 Group for $10 USD per month](https://buy.stripe.com/28E28s1b4gJcb2Y3Io5EY02). This is a voluntary recurring subscription for research and technical infrastructure, billed monthly until canceled. It does not purchase goods, special access, editorial influence or a tax-deductible charitable donation. The legal seller is identified at checkout; for billing or cancellation help, [contact the Group](https://561.group/contact/). [One-time tips](https://github.com/561-group/.github/blob/main/TIPS.md) use a separate rail.

**Buy practical work:** Have a bounded need for research, implementation, operations or public services? [Contact the Group](https://561.group/contact/) with the desired outcome, target date and a budget range. The appropriate existing service office can qualify scope, price, capacity and terms or advise a better alternative. An inquiry is not an accepted order.

- **Licensing.** A repository's own LICENSE file governs it. Indigenous cultural material is never put under an open
  license; see [Governance and Indigenous data](https://github.com/561-group/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md).
- **Security.** Report vulnerabilities privately from a repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**).
- **Tips.** [Send a tip](https://github.com/561-group/.github/blob/main/TIPS.md).
- **Contact.** https://561.group/contact/
