**The 561 Group.** The union of the four organizations below, operated by Morgan Sennhauser.

The 561 Group is operated by Morgan Sennhauser. Its work moves from science to engineering to operations to public
services, and each step has its own organization:

| Organization | Register | Default license |
|---|---|---|
| [Lenticule Science](https://github.com/lenticule-science) | Science: investigates what is possible, and publishes original investigations, sources, methods and reproducible results, supporting each claim to the level appropriate to its use. | MIT |
| [Red Cup Engineering](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering) | Engineering: makes the actual components, their composition boundaries, build inputs, tests and demonstrations independently reproducible. | MIT |

## Ways to participate economically

**Sustain public work:** [Support the 561 Group for $10 USD per month](https://buy.stripe.com/28E28s1b4gJcb2Y3Io5EY02). This is a voluntary recurring subscription for research and technical infrastructure, billed monthly until canceled. It does not purchase goods, special access, editorial influence or a tax-deductible charitable donation. The legal seller is identified at checkout; for billing or cancellation help, [contact the Group](https://561.group/contact/). [One-time tips](https://github.com/561-group/.github/blob/main/TIPS.md) use a separate rail.

**Buy practical work:** Have a bounded need for research, engineering, implementation, operations or public services? [Email a scoped service inquiry](mailto:emsenn@561.group?subject=%5B561%20Group%20sales%20inquiry%5D%20Request%20a%20scope%20and%20quote) with the desired outcome, target date, budget range if known, and a safe reply channel. You can also use the [existing private/public contact page](https://561.group/contact/). The appropriate existing seller office can qualify scope, price, capacity and terms or advise a better alternative. An inquiry is not an accepted order.

**Supply or subcontract:** If your organization can provide qualified delivery capacity, [email a supplier proposal](mailto:emsenn@561.group?subject=%5B561%20Group%20procurement%5D%20Supplier%20capacity%20proposal) identifying your actual services, availability, price basis and reply contact. The Group's existing procurement and delivery offices assess suitable opportunities; an email is not a purchase order.

**Read a published agent-operations book:** [Find *The Agent Operator's Handbook* in the Amazon Kindle catalog](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Agent+Operator%27s+Handbook+Causeway+Services&i=digital-text), using the Causeway Services imprint and the subtitle *A Field Guide to Dispatching, Steering, and Recovering Multiple AI Coding Agents at Once*. The publisher received a Kindle publication notice on September 30, 2026; the submitted US list price was $12. This links to a **retailer search, not a verified product checkout**—check the exact edition and current price before buying. The proposed direct EPUB/PDF purchase is not launched. [See the original Causeway book and course source](https://github.com/causeway-services/artificial-intelligence-curriculum-enterprise#readme).

**Try a real operating tool first:** [Get Causeway's free First Commission Worksheet](https://github.com/causeway-services/artificial-intelligence-curriculum-enterprise/blob/main/content/marketing/agent-operator-first-commission-workbook.md) to assign a bounded AI-agent job, handle overlapping workers, and check delivery. Teams that want guided practice can review the [existing five-session Multi-Agent Operating Discipline course](https://github.com/causeway-services/artificial-intelligence-curriculum-enterprise/blob/main/content/marketing/multi-agent-operating-discipline-for-teams.md) and [request a scoped training quote](mailto:emsenn@561.group?subject=%5B561%20Group%20sales%20inquiry%5D%20Agent%20operations%20training). Cohort dates and enrollment checkout have not been opened; the written course is an offer candidate, not a booking.
