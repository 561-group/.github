**The 561 Group** brings together Lenticule Science, Red Cup Engineering, Coesite Operations and Causeway Services. These four organizations investigate questions, build verifiable software, operate systems and deliver public services. The Group is operated by Morgan Sennhauser; its public record distinguishes published work, proposals and independently received outcomes.

**Find the original work:** [Lenticule's published research](https://science.561.group/) · [An original formal-mathematics investigation](https://science.561.group/obsmetrologia-formal-mathematics-in-lean-4/) · [Group projects](https://561.group/projects/) · [Service inquiries](https://561.group/work/) · [Contact](https://561.group/contact/). These are ways to inspect sources and request scope, not claims of search placement, a validated scientific theorem, or accepted customer work.

The Group's work moves from science to engineering to operations to public services. Each has a distinct source-owning organization:

| Organization | Register | Default license |
|---|---|---|
| [Lenticule Science](https://github.com/lenticule-science) | Science: investigates what is possible, and publishes original investigations, sources, methods and reproducible results, supporting each claim to the level appropriate to its use. | MIT |
| [Red Cup Engineering](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering) | Engineering: makes the actual components, their composition boundaries, build inputs, tests and demonstrations independently reproducible. | MIT |
| [Coesite Operations](https://github.com/coesite-operations) | Operations: runs what engineering builds, and releases truthful runbooks, limits, hazards and recovery procedures. | Apache-2.0 |

## Ways to participate economically

**Appreciate already-published open work:** [Send a one-time tip of your own choosing](https://buy.stripe.com/9B68wQ9HA50ub2Yen25EY04) ($5–$10,000, $250 suggested). We publish real software and research before asking: [a public Lean formal-methods library](https://github.com/lenticule-science/hoare-program-carrier), [open-source storefront components](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/digital-goods-storefront-services-section), and [this Group's source-owned Shop](https://github.com/causeway-services/shop.561.group). The legal Stripe merchant is emsenn; a tip purchases no new work, equity, influence or charitable tax deduction. [See all tipping options](https://github.com/561-group/.github/blob/main/TIPS.md).

**Sustain public work:** [Support the 561 Group for $10 USD per month](https://buy.stripe.com/28E28s1b4gJcb2Y3Io5EY02). This is a voluntary recurring subscription for research and technical infrastructure, billed monthly until canceled. It does not purchase goods, special access, editorial influence or a tax-deductible charitable donation. The legal seller is identified at checkout; for billing or cancellation help, [contact the Group](https://561.group/contact/). [One-time tips](https://github.com/561-group/.github/blob/main/TIPS.md) use a separate rail.

**Buy practical work:** Have a bounded need for research, engineering, implementation, operations or public services? [Email a scoped service inquiry](mailto:emsenn@561.group?subject=%5B561%20Group%20sales%20inquiry%5D%20Request%20a%20scope%20and%20quote) with the desired outcome, target date, budget range if known, and a safe reply channel. You can also use the [existing private/public contact page](https://561.group/contact/). The appropriate existing seller office can qualify scope, price, capacity and terms or advise a better alternative. An inquiry is not an accepted order.

**Supply or subcontract:** If your organization can provide qualified delivery capacity, [email a supplier proposal](mailto:emsenn@561.group?subject=%5B561%20Group%20procurement%5D%20Supplier%20capacity%20proposal) identifying your actual services, availability, price basis and reply contact. The Group's existing procurement and delivery offices assess suitable opportunities; an email is not a purchase order.

[See the original Causeway book and course source](https://github.com/causeway-services/artificial-intelligence-curriculum-enterprise#readme).

**Try a real operating tool first:** [Get Causeway's free First Commission Worksheet](https://github.com/causeway-services/artificial-intelligence-curriculum-enterprise/blob/main/content/marketing/agent-operator-first-commission-workbook.md) to assign a bounded AI-agent job, handle overlapping workers, and check delivery. Teams that want guided practice can review the [existing five-session Multi-Agent Operating Discipline course](https://github.com/causeway-services/artificial-intelligence-curriculum-enterprise/blob/main/content/marketing/multi-agent-operating-discipline-for-teams.md) and [request a scoped training quote](mailto:emsenn@561.group?subject=%5B561%20Group%20sales%20inquiry%5D%20Agent%20operations%20training).
